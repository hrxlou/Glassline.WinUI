# Trademark & Naming Policy

> Project policy for public naming and documentation. It is not legal advice.

## Project identity

**Glassline.WinUI** is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or distributed by Microsoft Corporation or Apple Inc.

The project logo, package names, sample application, and public marketing must use the independent **Glassline** identity. Third-party logos and product artwork are not part of project branding.

## Microsoft references

`WinUI`, `Windows`, `Windows App SDK`, and other Microsoft product names are used in plain text only to identify the technology/compatibility target.

Repository and package naming follows an independent-brand-first pattern (`Glassline.WinUI`) rather than presenting the project as an official Microsoft product. Do not use wording such as `official`, `certified`, `licensed`, or similar endorsement language unless an actual agreement grants that status.

Reference:
- https://www.microsoft.com/trademarks
- https://learn.microsoft.com/windows/apps/publish/partner-center/trademark-and-copyright-protection

## Apple references

Apple product/platform names such as `macOS`, `SwiftUI`, and `SF Symbols` may appear in research, comparison, compatibility, provenance, or non-goal documentation when necessary. They are not part of the Glassline product/package name.

Do not use Apple logos, Apple-owned graphic symbols, Apple product artwork, or wording that implies sponsorship/approval. Public Gallery content must remain an independent information architecture rather than an Apple application replica.

Reference:
- https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/trademark/appletmlist.html
- https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html

## Attribution

Where a release/document needs a general trademark notice, use a neutral form such as:

> Glassline.WinUI is an independent project. All third-party product names and trademarks are the property of their respective owners and are used only for identification or reference.

Specific attribution requirements for bundled third-party code/assets must be recorded in `THIRD-PARTY.yml` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES`.
